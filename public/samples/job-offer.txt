Subject: Offer of Employment as Senior Software Engineer

Dear Mr. Mehta,

We are pleased to offer you the position of Senior Software Engineer at TechCorp Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Company"), subject to the following terms and conditions:

1. COMMENCEMENT DATE
Your employment shall commence on 1st April 2024.

2. COMPENSATION
You will receive a fixed annual Cost to Company (CTC) of Rs. 18,00,000 (Rupees Eighteen Lakh Only). The breakdown of components shall be as determined by the Company from time to time in its sole discretion.

3. PROBATION
You will be on probation for a period of 6 months. During probation, either party may terminate employment with 7 days' notice. The Company may extend the probation period at its sole discretion without assigning any reason.

4. NOTICE PERIOD
After confirmation, you will be required to serve a notice period of 90 days ("Notice Period") upon resignation. In lieu of notice, you may pay the Company an amount equivalent to your 3 months' basic salary. The Company may terminate your employment with 30 days' notice or payment in lieu thereof.

5. SERVICE BOND
You acknowledge that the Company will invest significantly in your training and development. In consideration thereof, you agree to serve the Company for a minimum period of 2 years from the date of joining ("Bond Period"). In the event you resign or are terminated for cause before the completion of the Bond Period, you shall be liable to pay the Company a sum of Rs. 3,00,000 (Rupees Three Lakh Only) as liquidated damages for breach of this bond ("Bond Amount"). The Bond Amount shall be recoverable from any dues payable to you and/or through legal proceedings.

6. NON-COMPETE
During your employment and for a period of 24 months after cessation of your employment for any reason, you shall not, directly or indirectly: (a) engage in, consult for, or be employed by any company that competes with the Company's business anywhere in India; (b) solicit any employee of the Company to leave the Company; (c) solicit any client or prospective client of the Company. The Company shall have the right to seek injunctive relief for any breach of this clause.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All intellectual property created by you during your employment, whether during office hours or otherwise, whether using Company resources or personal resources, shall be the exclusive property of the Company. You hereby assign all such rights to the Company.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
You shall not, during or after employment, disclose any confidential information of the Company to any third party. This obligation shall survive termination of employment indefinitely.

9. WORKING HOURS
Your standard working hours shall be as determined by the Company. You may be required to work on weekends and public holidays without additional compensation, as overtime pay is included in your CTC.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This offer letter and the employment relationship shall be governed by the laws of India, and disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Bengaluru.

Please sign and return a copy of this letter within 5 days to indicate your acceptance of these terms.

Yours sincerely,
