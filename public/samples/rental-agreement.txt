PREMISES: Flat No. 204, 2nd Floor, Sunrise Towers, Andheri West, Mumbai - 400053 ("Premises")

1. TERM
This Agreement shall be for a period of 36 months commencing from 1st February 2024. The Licensee shall have no right to terminate this Agreement during the first 18 months ("Lock-in Period"). Termination during the Lock-in Period shall attract a penalty equal to 3 months' license fees.

2. LICENSE FEE
The Licensee shall pay a monthly license fee of Rs. 45,000 (Rupees Forty-Five Thousand Only) payable on or before the 5th day of each month. The Licensor reserves the right to increase the license fee by up to 15% annually without prior notice to the Licensee.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Licensee shall pay a refundable security deposit of Rs. 2,70,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Seventy Thousand Only), equivalent to 6 months' license fee, at the time of signing this Agreement. The deposit shall be refunded within 90 days of vacating the Premises, subject to deductions for damages, unpaid dues, and a non-refundable administrative fee of Rs. 25,000.

4. FORFEITURE OF DEPOSIT
The entire security deposit shall be forfeited without deduction if: (a) the Licensee terminates the agreement during the Lock-in Period; (b) the Licensee fails to give 3 months' advance written notice before vacating; (c) the Licensor, in his sole discretion, determines that the Premises have been used for any purpose other than residential use.

5. ENTRY AND INSPECTION
The Licensor or his authorized representative shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time and for any reason, including but not limited to inspection, repairs, or showing the property to prospective tenants, without prior notice to the Licensee.

6. MAINTENANCE
All maintenance costs, including plumbing, electrical repairs, painting, and pest control, shall be borne entirely by the Licensee, irrespective of the cause or nature of the damage.

7. RENEWAL
Upon expiry of this Agreement, renewal shall be at the sole discretion of the Licensor. The Licensor may refuse renewal without providing any reason. Any holding over after expiry shall be treated as a trespass and the Licensee shall be liable for double the monthly license fee for each month of holding over.

8. SUBLETTING
The Licensee shall not sublet, assign, or part with possession of the Premises or any part thereof under any circumstances.

9. UTILITIES
The Licensee shall be responsible for all utility charges including electricity, water, piped gas, and society maintenance charges. In case of default, the Licensor may disconnect utilities without notice.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes arising from this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Mumbai. The Licensee hereby waives any right to file a case in courts of any other jurisdiction.

11. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. Any oral assurances given by the Licensor or his agents are not binding.
